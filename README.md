Instead of waiting for stock to ship from hundreds of miles away, Booon treats your city’s premium physical retail landscape as its distributed warehouse.

### Direct Brand Store Pickup

Rather than relying solely on hidden warehouses, Booon’s system integrates directly with the live inventory of actual brand retail stores situated right inside your city’s top malls and high streets.

This model turns physical retail spaces into high-velocity dispatch nodes, ensuring that the latest styles hitting the physical shelves are instantly available for rapid delivery.

## 3. A Minute-by-Minute Breakdown of the 120-Minute Delivery Window

A two-hour delivery window leaves absolutely zero margin for human error, misplaced inventory, or transit delays.

### Delivery Timeline

| Time | Process |
|--------|---------|
| 00-05 Min | Live Store Mapping |
| 05-20 Min | In-Store Picking |
| 20-30 Min | Rider Pickup |
| 30-120 Min | Last-Mile Transit |

### Phase 1: Live Store Inventory Mapping (Minutes 00–05)

The moment your payment clears, the platform's order management layer instantly analyzes the geographic coordinates of your delivery address.

### Phase 2: In-Store Picking & Quality Control (Minutes 05–20)

Store executives receive a digital picking ticket and quickly retrieve the item from the store floor.

The garment undergoes a quality-control check before being packed into secure packaging.

### Phase 3: The Hyperlocal Rider Handshake (Minutes 20–30)

A nearby courier is dynamically matched to the assignment and picks up the parcel directly from the brand store.

### Phase 4: Navigating the Last Mile (Minutes 30–120)

Local riders utilize optimized routes and neighborhood knowledge to deliver the order quickly and efficiently.

## 5. Overcoming the Logistics Hurdles of Fashion Quick-Commerce

Delivering an outfit in two hours is vastly different from delivering groceries or hot meals.

### Garment Integrity

Clothes cannot simply be tossed into a delivery bag. They must remain completely clean, crisp, and unwrinkled during rapid transit.

In grocery delivery, a minor substitution might be acceptable. In fashion, a substitution is a failure.

If you order a medium-sized olive green co-ord set, a large size or a different shade of green will not work.

This requires 100% accurate, real-time inventory tracking integrated across every participating retail outlet.
